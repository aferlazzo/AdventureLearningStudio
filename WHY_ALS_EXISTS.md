# WHY ALS EXISTS

## The Founding Principles of Adventure Learning Studio

### Our Purpose

Every person accumulates knowledge throughout life.

Some learn through work. Some through raising families. Some through
service, hobbies, travel, failure, perseverance, and experience.

Much of that knowledge never reaches another person---not because it
lacks value, but because the people who possess it rarely think of
themselves as teachers.

Adventure Learning Studio exists to change that.

Its purpose is to help ordinary people transform what they know into
learning Adventures that others can learn from.

In doing so, ALS helps authors become better teachers themselves.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What We Believe

We believe everyone knows something worth teaching.

Teaching is not reserved for educators.

The greatest barrier is rarely knowledge. It is confidence.

ALS exists to remove that barrier.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Our Mission

Adventure Learning Studio helps people transform a lifetime of
experience into Adventures that others can learn from.

Authors do not need to know instructional design.

They only need something worth sharing.

ALS helps them build the rest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Learning While Doing

The best way to learn how to design learning experiences is by creating
them.

Every page should accomplish two things simultaneously:

-   Help the author make progress.
-   Help the author become more capable.

Learning is not separate from authoring.

Authoring is the learning experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Author Comes First

ALS begins with the author's purpose, not the software's features.

It asks:

> **What are you trying to help someone learn?**

Everything else follows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## One Step at a Time

Authors should never feel they must understand the entire journey before
beginning.

Every page should answer:

-   Why am I here?
-   What should I do next?
-   Why does it matter?

Confidence grows through meaningful progress.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## AI Is a Coach

Artificial intelligence is not the author.

It is the author's coach.

Its purpose is to help authors communicate experience more effectively.

Its success is measured by one question:

**Is the author becoming more capable?**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Every Feature Must Teach

Every feature has two responsibilities.

1.  Help create a better Adventure.
2.  Help create a better author.

If it only produces content, it is incomplete.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Confidence Before Complexity

Purpose comes before mechanics.

Guidance comes before instruction.

Progress comes before perfection.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Interface Is a Mentor

ALS does not describe itself.

It participates with the author.

Instead of saying:

> This page contains...

It says:

> Use this page to...

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Experience Is the Curriculum

Stories.

Mistakes.

Successes.

Failures.

Practical wisdom.

ALS helps organize experience into Adventures that others can understand
and remember.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Everyone Can Teach

Anyone who has learned something valuable has something worth teaching.

ALS exists to help people discover that they are capable of sharing what
they know.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Finished Product

When an Adventure is complete, two things should exist:

-   A learning Adventure.
-   A stronger author.

If the Adventure improved but the author did not, ALS has completed only
half of its mission.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Our Standard

Every design decision should answer:

-   Does this help someone share what they know?
-   Does this make the next step clearer?
-   Does this strengthen the author's confidence?
-   Does this help the author become a better teacher?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Future We Hope to Create

A world where valuable knowledge is no longer trapped inside a single
lifetime.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Our Promise

Every page should leave the author slightly more capable than when they
arrived.

Every author should leave believing:

> **I really can teach this.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## One Sentence

**Adventure Learning Studio exists so that valuable knowledge is never
trapped inside a single lifetime.**
