NORTH STAR
Purpose

Teach the practical car knowledge nobody ever taught you.

Audience

Ordinary people who own, borrow, rent, or drive cars.

Product

A collection of practical missions.

Promise

Every mission teaches one useful thing.

Outcome

Knowledge first.

Confidence follows.

Guiding Question

Would someone honestly say,

"Nobody ever taught me this."

If yes...

write the mission.

If no...

don't.

That's it.

One page.

If you ever get lost six months from now, you don't reread 40 pages of documentation.

You read NORTH_STAR.md in under a minute.

I actually think this may become the most valuable document in the project. Every successful long-term project has something like it: a concise statement of purpose that keeps the team from drifting. Even though you're a team of one, the same principle applies. When you're deciding whether to add a feature, write a mission, or redesign the website, NORTH_STAR.md gives you an immediate test: Does this help teach the practical car knowledge nobody ever taught you? If not, it probably doesn't belong.
