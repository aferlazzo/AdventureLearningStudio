# North Star

## Purpose

Teach the practical car knowledge nobody ever taught you.

## Audience

Ordinary people who own, borrow, rent, or drive cars.

## Promise

Every mission teaches one practical, immediately useful idea.

## Outcome

Increase knowledge.

Greater confidence is the natural result.

## Test

Before creating any mission, ask:

> Would someone honestly say,
> "Nobody ever taught me this."

If yes, it belongs.

If no, don't write it.

## Principle

Keep it practical.

Keep it approachable.

Keep it useful.

Everything else is secondary.
