# Adventure Learning Studio
# Author Workflow

## Purpose

Adventure Learning Studio guides authors from an initial idea to a published Learning Adventure.

The workflow should remain understandable, purposeful, and focused on the learner.

---

## Workflow

### 1. Create a New Adventure

Give the Adventure a working title and describe its subject.

### 2. Define the Learning Problem

Describe the real situation, difficulty, or need the Adventure will address.

### 3. Describe the Learner

Identify who the Adventure is for, what the learner may already know, and what may make the subject difficult.

### 4. Define the Learning Outcome

State what the learner should understand, decide, or be able to do after completing the Adventure.

The outcome should emphasize useful change rather than information alone.

### 5. Create Missions

Break the learning journey into focused Missions.

Each Mission should address one practical objective and prepare the learner for the next step.

### 6. Build the Storyboard

Arrange the Mission's situations, explanations, actions, and transitions in a clear sequence.

### 7. Add Visual Content

Use the visual form best suited to the subject, such as comic panels, photographs, diagrams, labels, illustrations, or demonstrations.

Visual content should clarify learning rather than decorate it.

### 8. Add Instructional Content

Add explanations, prompts, examples, practice, and any supporting material needed by the learner.

### 9. Preview

Review the Adventure as the learner will experience it.

Check clarity, sequence, cognitive load, visual usefulness, and whether each Mission supports the intended outcome.

### 10. Publish

Publish the Adventure in the appropriate format, such as web, print, seminar, classroom activity, mobile experience, or online course.

---

## Guiding Principle

The Studio should guide the author through this process rather than expose every editing feature immediately.

At every stage, the next meaningful step should be clear.
