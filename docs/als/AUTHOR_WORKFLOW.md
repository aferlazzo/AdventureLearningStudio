# Adventure Learning Studio
# Author Workflow

## Purpose

Adventure Learning Studio guides authors through creating effective learning experiences one step at a time.

Instead of exposing every editing feature immediately, the Studio leads authors through a natural workflow that mirrors the way people think about teaching.

---

# Workflow

## 1. Create a New Adventure

Create or open the learning project.

Examples:

- The Practical Car Knowledge Nobody Ever Taught You
- Workplace Safety
- Financial Literacy

An Adventure defines the subject being taught.

---

## 2. Define the Learning Problem

What problem does this Adventure solve?

Examples:

- People were never taught practical car knowledge.
- New employees don't understand workplace safety.
- Families struggle to manage a budget.

A clear problem provides direction for every mission that follows.

---

## 3. Describe the Learner

Who is this for?

Describe the learner rather than the topic.

Examples:

- First-time car owner
- Parent teaching a teenager
- New employee
- Homeowner

Understanding the learner helps determine the appropriate level of detail and presentation.

---

## 4. Define the Learning Outcome

What should the learner know or be able to do after completing the Adventure?

Possible outcomes include:

- Practical knowledge
- Confidence
- Competence
- Better decision-making
- Safer behavior
- New skills

The Adventure defines the outcome.

The Studio supports achieving it.

---

## 5. Create the Mission

Break the Adventure into small, manageable learning units.

Each mission should teach one meaningful idea or practical skill.

---

## 6. Build the Storyboard

Plan the learner's experience.

Define:

- situations
- sequence
- scenes
- interactions
- visual flow

The storyboard becomes the blueprint for the finished mission.

---

## 7. Add Visual Content

Create or organize the visuals needed to support learning.

Examples include:

- Comic panels
- Diagrams
- Photographs
- Illustrations
- Icons

Visuals should improve understanding rather than simply decorate the page.

---

## 8. Add Instructional Content

Complete the mission with instructional material.

Examples include:

- explanations
- warnings
- tips
- checklists
- questions
- actions
- summaries

Every piece of content should support the learning objective.

---

## 9. Preview

Review the mission as the learner will experience it.

Verify:

- clarity
- flow
- visuals
- navigation
- readability
- accuracy

---

## 10. Publish

Generate the finished learning product.

Possible outputs include:

- Website
- PDF
- Print
- Future publishing platforms

Authors create once.

Adventure Learning Studio handles publication.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Studio should guide authors through this workflow one step at a time.

Authors should always know:

- Where they are.
- What comes next.
- Why the current step matters.

If authors spend more time learning the software than creating effective learning experiences, the workflow should be simplified.
