# Adventure Learning Studio Glossary

## Adventure

A complete guided learning experience organized around an overall learning outcome.

## Mission

A focused learning unit within an Adventure. Each Mission addresses one practical objective and moves the learner toward the Adventure's overall outcome.

## Story

A meaningful sequence of situations or events used to give learning purpose and context.

## Storyboard

The planned sequence of scenes, panels, explanations, learner actions, and transitions within a Mission.

## Scene

A distinct situation, moment, or setting within a Mission.

## Panel

A single visual unit within a storyboard. A panel may contain an illustration, photograph, diagram, text, dialogue, or other visual content.

## Dialogue

Words spoken or thought by characters within a visual story.

## Asset

Any reusable file used by an Adventure, including images, photographs, diagrams, audio, video, or documents.

## Preview

A representation of the Adventure as the learner will experience it before publication.

## Publish

To produce and make an Adventure available in a delivery format such as web, print, seminar, classroom activity, mobile experience, or online course.

## Guided Workspace

An authoring experience that leads the author through one meaningful decision or task at a time.

## Advanced Workspace

An authoring experience that provides direct access to more detailed controls and editing options.

## Learning Outcome

The useful change an Adventure is designed to produce in the learner, such as knowledge, understanding, competence, confidence, safer behavior, better decisions, or a new skill.

## Workspace

The area of Adventure Learning Studio where an author plans, creates, reviews, and publishes an Adventure.

## Learning Product

A learner-facing result created through the Studio, such as a website, printed guide, seminar, classroom activity, mobile experience, or online course.

## Author

A person who uses Adventure Learning Studio to create a Learning Adventure.

## Learner

A person who participates in or uses a Learning Adventure.

---

The Studio defines **how** learning products are created.

Each Adventure defines **what** is taught.
