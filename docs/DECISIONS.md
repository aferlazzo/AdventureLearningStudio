# Design Decisions

## Guided Workspace

Decision:
The Guided Workspace is the default authoring experience.

Reason:
New authors should think about learners rather than software.

---

## Advanced Workspace

Decision:
Keep the traditional editor.

Reason:
Experienced authors need direct access.

---

## Comic-first authoring

Decision:
Comic panels are first-class objects.

Reason:
Visual storytelling improves learning.
