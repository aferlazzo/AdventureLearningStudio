Adventure Learning Conversation Specification

Version 1.0

Purpose

The purpose of Adventure Learning Studio is not to collect information.

Its purpose is to help an author transform a real experience into a reusable learning adventure.

The Studio accomplishes this through a structured conversation.

Each conversation has one objective.

Each question has one purpose.

The author should never wonder:

"Why is it asking me this?"

Conversation 1 — Discover the Adventure
Objective

Identify a real experience worth teaching.

Studio Introduction

Every Adventure Learning project begins with something that actually happened.

Don't worry about writing lessons yet.

Just tell me about the moment.

Question

What happened?

Examples

A warning light came on.
I borrowed my parents' car.
A box fell off a truck.
I couldn't find the gas door.
Success Criteria

The Studio understands

the event
the setting
why this is a real experience
Conversation 2 — Discover the Anxiety
Objective

Identify the uncertainty.

Studio Introduction

Good adventures begin with uncertainty.

Question

What made this stressful?

Examples

I didn't know what to do.
I was afraid I'd damage the car.
I didn't know whether it was safe.
Success Criteria

The Studio can finish this sentence:

The learner worries that ______.

Conversation 3 — Discover the Decision

(Our new addition.)

Objective

Identify the decision that creates learning.

Studio Introduction

Every Adventure includes a decision.

Question

What decision had to be made?

Examples

Stop now or continue?
Call for help?
Keep driving?
Read the owner's manual?
Success Criteria

The Studio understands the choice.

Conversation 4 — Capture Experience
Objective

Capture practical wisdom.

Question

What did the experienced person do?

Examples

Pulled off safely.
Inspected the tires.
Looked underneath.
Called roadside assistance.

Second Question

Why?

This is where experience becomes teachable.

Conversation 6 — Build Capability
Objective

Define the learning outcome.

Question

When this Adventure is over...

What should the learner confidently know how to do?

Example

Decide whether it is safe to continue driving after hitting road debris.

Conversation 7 — Build the Story

Only now does storytelling begin.

Questions

Why today?

What happened next?

The Studio assembles the narrative automatically.

Studio Design Rules

I think these rules should be posted on the wall while we build the software.

Ask one question at a time.
Always explain why you're asking.
Show examples before asking for input.
Never overwhelm the author with empty fields.
Teach Adventure Learning by using Adventure Learning.
Always help the author discover the next step.
Generate structure whenever possible instead of asking the author to format it.
The Studio is a coach, not a form.
One final thought

When we started this conversation, we were designing software.

I don't think that's what we're doing anymore.

We're designing the behavior of an expert mentor.

The interface, database, and AI model are replaceable over time.

But the conversation—the way the Studio guides someone from a real experience to a polished learning adventure—is the lasting intellectual property.

If we protect and refine that conversation, everything else can evolve around it.
