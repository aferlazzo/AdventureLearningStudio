Design Constitution v1
Our Mission
Help people transform their expertise into Adventures by teaching while they author.

Principle 1
Confidence is the product.

Everything else supports confidence.

Principle 2
Every page answers four questions.

Why am I here?
Where am I?
What should I do next?
How do I leave this page?
If a page fails one of these, it isn't finished.

Principle 3
Authors should never feel lost.

Confusion is a bug.

Hesitation is a bug.

Too many choices are a bug.

Principle 4
Teach in the moment.

Don't send people to a manual.

Teach exactly what they need exactly when they need it.

Principle 5
Coach, don't criticize.

Never say

Error.

Instead say

Here's why this matters.

Principle 6
Remove before adding.

If removing something makes the page clearer...

remove it.

Principle 7
One screen. One purpose.

Every screen exists to accomplish one thing well.

Principle 8
Every interaction should increase confidence.

When someone leaves a page they should feel

"I know what to do next."

Principle 9
Track Confidence Bugs.

We don't just fix broken software.

We fix uncertainty.

Principle 10
Teaching while you author.

If a feature doesn't help teach the author...

question why it exists.
